About the project:

✦

As stated in my intro, I got my start in video games ever since 2006, but my true programming start was in 2018 making mods for various a game called Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes made by Steel Crate Games. It gave me an opportunity to take my small first steps into learning Unity, as there was an amazing modkit made by the community and I wouldn't need to create an entire game from scratch with no knowledge.

My first released mod was Module Homework, a puzzle that revolved around trivia of other modules made by other people. Uploaded to the Workshop in September of 2018, it was a pretty rocky start with very buggy and messy code, and the experience required a cheat sheet in order for the experience and challenge to be fair.

But one year later in 2019, I created what I still call my best and favorite mod I've ever worked on. I took the game's bomb, shrunk it down, and put 5 micro-modules on it to create... "Micro-Modules"! Not only was it a lot more fun to work on, it was the first mod that broke the 50 favorites limit on Steam, and over 7000 subscribers, with 19 positive reviews to 1 negative.

For now, I've mostly taken a break from the game to focus on other projects, though I do still have some ideas scribbled down just in case I want to return at some point.

✦

What I learnt from the Journey:

✦
  • The basics of Unity: how the engine works, how to model stuff with basic shapes, and how to use scripts to make those shapes function.
  • Iterative Process: taking critism and feedback from the fellow players who've tried my creations, and incorporating it into new and improved versions.
  • A little bit of moddeling: in later mods I was working on, I tried my hand at Blender to make some actual models for my mods, instead of relying on assets online.

About the project:

🟆

A game set in a modernized distant future, you're part of a anti-cyberterrorist squad tasked with protecting the city from malicious hacker groups. You and your team are sent out to deal with an physical attack on the HQ housing the central servers, and prevent them from shutting down the grid, before being framed for the attack and designated as outlaws.

This was the first project I did for my studies in Semester 4 Game Design. The project was so well received by the assessors that I was invited to show it at the Fontys ICT Open Day 16 March 2024, as advertising for the Fontys Game Design Technologies semester. Other people were allowed to play the game, and the reception for the game was really positive, with one person almost beating the first mission in the allowed time.

🟆

What I learnt from the Journey:

🟆
  • Game Design Fundamentals:I used this project to learn about the basics of Game Design, such as making Game Design Documents, doing my own MDA research, and how to use those elements to make interesting game elements.
  • NPC/Bot Behavior: for my enemies, I had to learn how to use Unity's NavMesh package, and how to utilize State Machines to make challenging enemies for the player to combat.

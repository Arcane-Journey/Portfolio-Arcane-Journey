About the project:

🟆

Go spelunking in an ancient pyramid tomb, where the rooms alternate between high intensity exercises and mysterious challenges. Train your body while running deeper into the caverns below, and your brain while solving the ancient puzzles

This game is a roguelike dungeon crawlerin VR, based on the concept of HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training) which switches between rigorous exercises and short moments of rest. We made this in VR to really get players immersed in the training, with the VR's hand and head tracking as the main controls.

The end product was shown at a tech market at school, where students could try out the products other students had made. The reception was mixed leaning to positive, and we had taken the constructive critisism to further improve the product.

🟆

What I learnt from the Journey:

🟆
  • Working with Meta VR: we chose the Meta Quest 2 for this assignment as it was most readily available, but this meant we had to learn how to use Meta's own XR libraries in order to get the game working on the headset alongside the controls and interactions.
  • Puzzle Design: since I was tasked with making the resting areas, I had to come up with interesting puzzles that would be easy to understand and would not take longer than a couple minutes of rest.

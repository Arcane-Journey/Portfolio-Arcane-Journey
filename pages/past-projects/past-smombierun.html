A game that created a real world event

🟆

About the project:

🟆

Picture this: you're walking down the street, and suddenly someone bumps into you while they're looking down at their phone and not paying attention. They're like zombies constantly on their phones, or basically, a "Smombie"!

The SmombieRun was an initiative by Fontys Hogescholen, led by Rens van der Vorst, to create awareness of unsafe phone usage in public. And what better way to do that then to organize a marathon event where people must stay busy on their phones, all with a nice zombie theme to go with it?

The goal is to run a certain amount of laps or distance, all while completing minigames and tasks on your phone. If you make a mistake, you gain a strike. Three strikes, and you're out of the race.

The event was held on 2 July 2026, and was covered by multiple news organizations such as Eindhovens Dagblad, Omroep Brabant, and Brabants Dagblad. You can find the sources at the bottom of the page.

🟆

What I learnt from the Journey:

🟆
  • ASP.NET Webservices: in order to keep track of the ongoing events, and make sure game sessions run synchronous, I learnt how to set up my own ASP.NET webservice with built-in database to store sessions and handle traffic via WebSockets.
  • PurrNet Multiplayer: on the Client side, I used PurrNet (an open-source networking library) to communicate with my ASP.NET services, being able to create, join, start and end sessions from the respective devices.

    The devices were then split into the "Organizer" and the "Players" depending on who made the server.
